How to try it end-to-end
1. Open the Patient flow, flip on “Smile simulation,” complete a consult.
2. Open the Practice console — your submission appears at the top of the queue (🔥 hot).
3. Open it, record a video against the AI script, send it, then preview the patient closing-page.

Data is shared via your browser's local storage. Use Reset demo in the console to restore the seeded queue.
