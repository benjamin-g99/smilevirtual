How to try it end-to-end
1. Patient flow → flip on “Smile simulation,” complete a consult.
2. Practice console → your lead is top of the queue (🔥). Tweak their sim, open the Consult Studio, record, send.
3. Patient portal → watch/share/message. Check Performance & Settings in the console.

Data is shared via your browser's local storage. Use Reset demo in the console to restore the seeded data.
